Hosting and security

This website uses Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) for delivery and protection against attacks. It processes IP address, time, requested address and browser identification. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in a secure, available service. Cloudflare processes connection data to provide and secure its services; retention depends on the security purpose and contractual deletion rules. Technically necessary security cookies may be used under § 165(3) TKG 2021. Cloudflare is a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). For transfers to the USA, Cloudflare relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), supplemented by standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Details: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

My Own Server and Server Logs

My server is located in a data centre of dataflair (Kurz & Lankow GbR, Hochkönigstraße 2, 83395 Freilassing, Germany), which operates it on my behalf. Whenever a page on this server is requested, the web server writes a log entry with the IP address, time, requested address, status code, amount of data transferred, referring page and browser identification. I need these entries to find errors and fend off attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are rotated daily and normally deleted within 15 days unless an entry is needed to investigate a specific attack.

WinMorph

The website stores only the language you explicitly choose under “wm-lang” in browser storage until you clear it. This provides your requested language setting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; § 165(3) TKG 2021). Release details are fetched from /api/release without saving them in local storage. Downloads and update requests from the installed app use this domain and generate the connection data and server logs described above.

Contact by Email

When you write to me, I process your email address, your name and the content of your message in order to reply. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns a specific project, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The messages are stored on my own mail server at dataflair (Kurz & Lankow GbR, Hochkönigstraße 2, 83395 Freilassing, Germany). I delete them once the matter is settled, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

Your Rights

These rights apply subject to their statutory conditions. You may withdraw consent at any time for the future. If you believe I am not processing your data lawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority. In Austria, this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb.gv.at. There is no automated decision-making or profiling.
